# POWERFARM Negative Constraint Library

Use these as plain-language exclusions. They are intentionally platform-neutral.

## Always avoid when inappropriate
- generic stock-photo posing
- exaggerated smiles
- fake handshakes
- neon cyberpunk color
- cyan-magenta lighting
- holograms
- fantasy lightning
- decorative electrical sparks
- excessive glow
- overprocessed HDR
- plastic CGI surfaces
- impossible equipment
- warped machinery
- incoherent cables
- fake unreadable labels
- nonsense dashboards
- futuristic interfaces with no function
- fantasy architecture
- excessively clean industrial environments
- fake greenwashing symbolism
- random leaves floating around machinery
- cartoonish sustainability imagery
- unrealistic PPE
- cloned people
- extra fingers or malformed hands
- impossible reflections
- oversaturated skies
- exaggerated lens flare
- heavy teal-orange grading
- rainbow color palettes
- glossy luxury-advertising styling
- game-art aesthetics

## Composition exclusions
- no clear focal point
- visual clutter
- centered object with no hierarchy
- busy pattern directly behind headline space
- cramped crop with no room for brand typography
- tiny infrastructure with no sense of scale
- excessive depth-of-field blur that hides technical context
