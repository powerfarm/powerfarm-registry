# POWERFARM Universal Image Prompt Library

These prompts are intentionally **model-agnostic**. They avoid platform-specific parameters and can be adapted to most generative image systems.

---

## 1. Infrastructure Hero

Create a premium editorial photograph for POWERFARM showing a large-scale energy infrastructure site at first or last light. Use strong structural perspective, credible engineering detail, deep charcoal shadows, restrained warm highlights, and generous negative space for a headline. The scene should feel operational, modern, precise, and physically plausible. Keep color saturation controlled. Include subtle amber accents only where they could realistically occur in lighting, indicators, or reflections. Avoid science-fiction architecture, excessive glow, generic corporate stock-photo styling, impossible equipment, and oversaturated skies.

Best for:
- website heroes
- campaign openers
- presentation covers
- annual reports

---

## 2. Solar Generation

Create a realistic POWERFARM image of a utility-scale solar installation integrated into a believable landscape. Emphasize geometry, repetition, scale, clean engineering, and the relationship between infrastructure and environment. Use natural directional light, realistic panel reflections, controlled contrast, and subtle atmospheric depth. Keep the palette dark, neutral, and warm rather than bright blue or highly saturated. Leave one area of clean negative space for typography. Avoid unrealistic mirror-like panels, exaggerated lens flare, fake greenwashing imagery, and idyllic lifestyle advertising.

Best for:
- renewable energy pages
- sustainability reports
- investor materials
- social campaigns

---

## 3. Technician / Engineer

Create a documentary-style POWERFARM portrait of a skilled technician or engineer actively working with real industrial or electrical equipment. The subject should appear focused, capable, and naturally engaged with the task. Use believable PPE where appropriate, realistic tools, authentic surfaces, directional industrial or natural light, and shallow-to-medium depth of field. Keep the image grounded, premium, and unscripted. Avoid staged handshake imagery, exaggerated smiles, spotless fake workwear, impossible interfaces, and generic office stock photography.

Best for:
- careers
- operations
- service
- case studies
- leadership-in-the-field stories

---

## 4. Facility Interior

Create a high-end architectural and industrial photograph for POWERFARM inside a modern energy, battery, control, or electrical facility. Emphasize clean geometry, cable management, modular engineering, technical order, scale, and controlled lighting. Use neutral dark tones, steel, concrete, subtle warm highlights, and realistic practical lights. The environment should feel advanced because it is well engineered, not because it looks futuristic. Avoid neon sci-fi corridors, excessive screens, glowing holograms, and implausible machinery.

Best for:
- operations
- technology pages
- technical decks
- reports

## 5. Equipment Detail

Create a close technical photograph for POWERFARM focusing on a real-looking component, connection, control surface, cable system, inverter, switchgear, battery module, or engineered mechanism. Make texture, tolerances, materials, fasteners, labels, and wear feel believable. Use strong side light and controlled shadows. Compose the object with graphic clarity and enough negative space for captions. Include amber only as a small functional indicator or reflection. Avoid macro fantasy, fake interfaces, decorative sparks, and impossible mechanical geometry.

Best for:
- product/service details
- technical documentation
- social crops
- presentation dividers

The scene should communicate operational intelligence, reliability, and calm control. Avoid hacker-room clichés, neon blue lighting, giant holograms, unreadable code walls, and cyberpunk aesthetics.

Create an abstract POWERFARM campaign image inspired by controlled electrical energy and engineered flow, without depicting fantasy lightning or magical energy. # Prompt Assembly Formula

For new imagery, combine:

**[Subject] + [Environment] + [Action] + [Camera/Composition] + [Lighting] + [Material Realism] + [POWERFARM Color Discipline] + [Negative Constraints] + [Intended Use]**

Example:

Create a realistic image of a field engineer inspecting a utility-scale battery installation at dusk, photographed from a low three-quarter angle with the equipment creating strong geometric lines. Use believable PPE, natural working posture, practical industrial lighting, deep neutral shadows, restrained amber indicator lights, realistic metal and cable textures, and clean negative space on the left for a headline. The scene should feel documentary, premium, technically credible, and calm. Avoid stock-photo posing, cyberpunk lighting, fake interfaces, impossible equipment, excessive glow, and oversaturation. Intended for a POWERFARM website hero.
