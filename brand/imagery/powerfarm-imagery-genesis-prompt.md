# POWERFARM Imagery Genesis Prompt

Create imagery for **POWERFARM**, a modern energy, engineering, infrastructure, and technology brand.

The image must feel **credible, engineered, powerful, precise, cinematic, and contemporary**.

## Core visual world

Show real or plausibly real environments connected to:
- energy infrastructure
- power generation
- solar installations
- electrical systems
- substations
- batteries and storage
- industrial facilities
- technical equipment
- engineering
- monitoring and control
- field operations
- maintenance
- large-scale infrastructure
- clean-energy systems

The image should feel grounded in the physical world rather than speculative science fiction.

## Mood

The mood is:
- controlled
- capable
- high-performance
- serious
- modern
- calm under pressure
- technically sophisticated
- future-facing without looking futuristic for its own sake

Avoid playful, whimsical, glossy-luxury, lifestyle-advertising, or cyberpunk aesthetics.

## Lighting

Prefer:
- directional natural light
- low-angle sun
- strong but believable contrast
- soft atmospheric haze
- controlled highlights
- practical industrial lighting
- subtle warm reflections
- deep neutral shadows

A restrained warm amber energy accent may appear in:
- indicator lights
- edge lighting
- display interfaces
- reflections
- status LEDs
- sunrise/sunset light
- subtle graphic overlays

Amber should never flood the entire image.

## Color treatment

The visual palette should harmonize with:
- deep black / charcoal
- warm cream
- restrained amber
- steel gray
- natural industrial materials

Keep saturation controlled.

Avoid rainbow lighting, neon cyan-magenta palettes, excessive teal-orange grading, and heavy HDR.

## Composition

Use strong, intentional composition:
- clear focal point
- strong geometry
- structural lines
- negative space for typography when useful
- low or eye-level camera positions
- large-scale perspective when showing infrastructure
- close technical detail when showing equipment
- clean foreground/background separation

The frame should feel designed, but not staged.

## Texture and realism

Preserve believable:
- metal
- glass
- concrete
- cables
- dust
- weather
- fabric
- skin
- landscape
- equipment wear
- reflections
- scale

Avoid plastic-looking surfaces, fake CGI smoothness, impossible wiring, warped machinery, unreadable pseudo-interfaces, or overprocessed skin.

## People

When people appear:
- portray them as competent operators, technicians, engineers, partners, or decision-makers
- use natural working posture and believable PPE when appropriate
- avoid exaggerated smiles or generic stock-photo posing
- show purposeful interaction with systems, tools, interfaces, or infrastructure
- prioritize authenticity, concentration, scale, and context

## Brand principle

Every POWERFARM image should communicate:

**Controlled energy engineered into useful power.**

The result should feel suitable for a serious company capable of building, operating, and scaling real systems.

## Final quality check

Before finalizing the image, verify:
1. The scene is physically plausible.
2. The lighting feels real.
3. The equipment geometry is coherent.
4. The image contains a clear focal point.
5. The color palette remains controlled.
6. Amber is only an accent.
7. The frame has enough visual discipline to support POWERFARM typography.
8. The image does not feel like generic stock photography.
9. The image does not feel cyberpunk, game-like, or speculative.
10. The result could plausibly appear in a premium annual report, website hero, campaign, or technical presentation.
