# POWERFARM Graphic Elements System

This kit contains reusable vector supporting elements for POWERFARM.

## Design principle

The graphics should feel like **controlled energy translated into structure**.

Use sharp geometry, diagonal movement, technical framing, grids, and restrained amber accents. A composition should normally feature one dominant graphic gesture rather than many competing effects.

## Important

These shapes are **supporting brand graphics**, not alternate logos.

Do not use the energy bolt, angular bracket, or any other supporting element as a replacement for the official POWERFARM emblem.

## Included elements

1. Energy bolt
2. Double slash
3. Angular bracket
4. Technical corners
5. Energy-flow lines
6. Dot matrix
7. Technical grid
8. Directional chevrons
9. Energy bars
10. Diagonal field

## Usage rules

- Prefer POWERFARM Cream on POWERFARM Black.
- Use Energy Amber as a restrained point of emphasis.
- Keep joins crisp and geometry intentional.
- Cropping is encouraged for large background graphics.
- Do not add bevels, glows, shadows, metallic effects, or random gradients.
- Do not rotate individual motifs arbitrarily. Reuse a small family of consistent diagonal angles.
- Keep enough negative space for typography and the official logo.
- Use one dominant motif per composition.

## v0.3 correction

All 10 elements were hard-coded cream, so amber could not punctuate the system
without editing files. They now use `fill="currentColor"` with a cream
presentation-attribute default, driven by `.pf-graphic--accent` and siblings.
